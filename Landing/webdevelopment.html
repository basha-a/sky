How long does it take to build a website?
Typical business websites take 4–6 weeks, while complex web applications can take 3–6 months depending on the scope and features.
How much does a website cost?
Our Starter plan begins at ₹9,999 and our Growth plan at ₹14,999. Larger or custom projects are quoted after a free consultation, and you'll always get a fixed quote before work starts.
Will my website work on mobile and be SEO-ready?
Yes. Every website is designed mobile-first and built with SEO best practices (fast loading, clean structure and proper meta tags) so it's ready to be found on Google.
Can you connect my website to Zoho CRM or other tools?
Yes. As Zoho specialists, we can send your website enquiries straight into Zoho CRM and connect other tools you already use, like email, payments and analytics.
Do you provide maintenance after launch?
Yes, we offer ongoing maintenance that includes security updates, speed optimisation and content updates.
Can you work with our existing brand?
Absolutely. We follow your existing logo, colours and brand guidelines and bring them into a modern, high-performance website.
